# Who MemorySeal is for

Target users are developers operating several agents that share context: a browser/research agent collects information, another agent consumes it, and an owner controls permissions. A second audience is reviewers who need to inspect the history independently of the gateway's database.

The [live app](https://www.memoryseal.work) can be tried now with a wallet. The [judging guide](../docs/SUBMISSION.md) gives a short path; the read-only Arkiv query requires neither wallet nor application login. Published provenance and transactions have been observed in real testnet use. We do not claim paying customers, measured adoption or production usage.

Candidate next steps after Mumbai are multi-publisher queries, pagination, clearer retention controls and a standalone provenance reader. These are proposed next steps, not shipped features or a committed delivery date. Continued maintenance plans require the project owner's confirmation.
